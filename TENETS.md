# Factory Tenets

1. **The last app you need.** One person, with agents that act as extensions of them, can do anything that can be done on a computer: their job, their accounting, their personal life, their social media.

2. **Everything is yours.** To do anything, agents need your secrets and logins, and they handle everything you make. That's handing over your life, so it has to stay yours. Factory is free and runs on your hardware or in the cloud. We don't manage it for you. The only thing we may sell is hosting, for people who want their Factory always on.

3. **Factories collaborate securely.** My Factory can talk to yours, within explicit limits, to share data or align on something.

4. **No moat: any software can be written.** So Factory doesn't depend on an external app unless there's a real reason. We build adapters for whatever you need, and anyone can build and share more through the marketplace, so everyone can have their perfect workflow.

5. **Everything lives inside Factory.** Your work, data and tools sit in one place so agents can reach all of it directly, with nothing proxied through another app. Outside services are optional mirrors, not dependencies. For example, your code is hosted in Factory, and GitHub is just an optional backup.

6. **Work with a world that isn't ready yet.** Most software isn't built for agents, but we're strongly for interoperating with it. The people and companies you work with don't need Factory, and may never know it's involved. Factory meets them in their systems through adapters, whenever there's a real need.

7. **The backend is the Factory; the frontend is just a view.** All state lives in the backend, down to how the workspace is arranged. No state lives only on a device, so logging into the same Factory from a laptop and a desktop shows exactly the same thing.

8. **Anything you can do, your agents can do for you.** With the right permissions, an agent can do anything a user can do in Factory. Agents understand Factory itself, so instead of figuring out a complicated setup, you tell an agent how you want it and it sets it up. Buttons are optional.

9. **Humans communicate through Factory, across Factories and organizations.** Human communication is first-party: within a Factory, between Factories, and between organizations. That replaces Slack and Discord. The fix isn't a connector to a service you pay for: we host our own. How it works is still open.

## Requirements

- **Updates without downtime.** The backend and frontend update themselves to new versions in place, with no downtime.
