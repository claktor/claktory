# Security

Claktory runs on your own computer, holds your keys and lets your devices reach it from anywhere, so a security problem in it matters. Thank you for telling us about one.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@claktory.com**. Please don't open a public issue or post about it before it's fixed.

Tell us:

- what you found, and what someone could do with it;
- the version of Claktory (in the app's Settings, beside Check for updates);
- the steps to show it, as short as you can make them.

We'll confirm we got your report, keep you posted while we fix it, and say when the fix is out. If you'd like to be thanked by name in the release notes, say so.

## Testing

- Test only against your own Claktory, on your own computers and devices.
- Never against someone else's Claktory, and never against claktory.com's services (the account service, the relays) beyond what using Claktory normally does.
- Don't read, keep or change data that isn't yours. Showing that something is reachable is enough: stop there and write to us.

## Supported versions

Fixes go into the next release. Claktory offers each release in the app, and you choose when to update, so please update to the latest before reporting.
