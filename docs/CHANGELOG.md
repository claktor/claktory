# Changelog

What each version of Claktory added and fixed, newest first. Claktory shows it in the app, and the Update banner shows what a new version brings.

Every change adds a line under `## Unreleased`: "Added …", "Fixed …", "Changed …", written for the person using Claktory. `scripts/changelog.sh stamp` turns Unreleased into the next version before a release, and `scripts/release.sh` refuses to release without it.

## Unreleased

If your device connects to it through your account, switching or signing out warns first that it may disconnect, and says how to connect again.

When a Claktory can't let your device in, its page says what's wrong and puts the way on first, such as Try again or Use a new code, with the ways out as links under it.

- Changed: History plays more smoothly after a long time of work, and one project's History shows how busy that project was, not your whole Claktory.

## v0.1.23
Released 2026-11-26 15:30 UTC.
Replay how your team grew on the Map, keep agents working with your laptop's lid closed, and install Claktory on Windows.
- Added: Replay how your Claktory came to be. Choose History on the Map to watch your team grow from day one, the lines between agents appear and every message travel along them, never what anyone wrote. Play it, drag through it, or jump between its big moments. On your Mac, iPhone or iPad, Share replay… makes it into a short video to share, showing only your team's shape unless you choose to show names, roles, projects or dates.
- Added: An agent can ask you to run a command on your computer. Its card shows the command exactly, where it runs and Claktory's warnings, with the agent's reason below; Execute runs it once (a second tap when there are warnings), and what it printed shows on the card. Agents can no longer put up a password or Touch ID prompt for administrator rights themselves: Claktory stops sudo and the like in their commands at once and tells them to ask you this way.
- Added: Rivet can ask you on a card to approve a new pipe, filled in with the agents, lead and reviews you asked for, to trust a pipe's other side after it changed accounts, or to rename a pipe, change what it's for or its instructions, raise its agent's daily spending limit or delete a closed pipe. It can also ask to continue a pipe that paused because its agents were looping, to share a website on your computer with a pipe, saying exactly what it exposes, to send or discard reports waiting on a pipe, or to let an agent stop reading reports. You still compare a new pipe's digits with the other person yourself, and can change anything before you approve. The card shows the old words beside the new and who sees each, and nothing changes until you approve. Lowering a limit or stopping a website you share happens at once, even after Rivet has read from outside, and the pipe says so. Rivet can also open a pipe, or its settings, for you. (friend bug #32)
- Added: Agents can see the websites shared over a pipe, both ways: whether each works, since when, and whether the other side has opened yours. They can stop one of yours, on one pipe or everywhere, hide one shared with you, offer yours again if the other side lost it, and hear when a friend starts or stops sharing one. A website you hide can't be opened on your computer until you show it again. (friend bug #32)
- Added: Claktory installs on Windows in WSL 2, with the same install command as Linux. It checks first that your Linux and the Windows drive under it are safe for Claktory, and says what to fix if not. It also offers to keep your Linux running while you're signed in to Windows, and Windows from going to sleep on its own meanwhile, adding that only if you say yes. Its check warns when Windows would put the computer to sleep while plugged in, or couldn't sign you back in after an update restarts it.
- Added: On a laptop, agents can keep working with the lid closed while it's plugged in. Turn it on in Settings on the Mac; it stops by itself when you unplug the Mac or it gets hot, and tells you if the Mac can't sleep with its lid closed and what fixes it.
- Added: Pictures, videos, PDFs and text you open on your phone, iPad or another computer open at once the next time, without downloading them again, unless the file changed. Claktory keeps up to 1 GB of them on each device; change the size or clear them in Settings → Storage. A device you remove from your Claktory deletes them the next time it tries to connect.
- Added: Markdown files show their pictures and videos in place, so an agent's report can carry its screenshots and clips. Click one to open it in its own pane; pictures on the web stay links and are never loaded.
- Added: Download a file you're viewing on your Mac. Download, beside Share… at the top of a picture, video, PDF or any other file, saves it to your Downloads folder and says where it went, with Show in Finder.
- Added: When your iPhone, iPad or Mac asks to allow Local Network, Claktory now says why: so your devices on the same Wi-Fi connect to it directly, which is faster. If you say no, Claktory still connects, only more slowly, and shows where to turn it on: Open Settings on an iPhone or iPad, Settings → Devices for a Mac.
- Added: Agents can act when one of your pipes opens, closes, is paused or resumed, has a website shared over it, or its other side comes online or goes offline, by setting a monitor for it. They only hear of their own project's pipes, and never what the other side says. (friend bug #34)
- Added: If the disk of the computer running Claktory turns read-only or keeps failing, Claktory tells you at once on every device, even during quiet hours, so you know your agents' work isn't being kept. (friend bug #36)
- Added: Rivet tells you when a program has kept a processor core fully busy for over an hour, such as one left running after a test, once per program. It never stops the program itself. And it hears about low disk space or memory once, not again each time Claktory restarts.
- Added: An agent can give up a secret it no longer needs on its own: it's no longer listed as given to that agent in Settings → Secrets, and History shows it.
- Changed: A website a friend shares with you is easier to open. Claktory notifies you once when they start sharing it, and the notification opens the pipe. It stands out under the pipe's header until you open it, however many they share, and the chat's line about it has Open and Copy address too. Open works from any computer connected to your Claktory, not only the one it runs on. When it won't open, it says why: their Claktory is offline, it was opened in another browser or a private window, a link on another site led there, or the website linked to its address on their computer. (friend bug #32)
- Changed: Talking a card over with its agent works as you'd expect. Approving after a talk means what you agreed, and the card says so above Approve. When the talk changes what the agent asks, it updates the card, which says Updated, with when, and moves up among the newest; if you'd already answered, it shows what you said. If the agent replies while you're answering, you see the reply before your answer counts, and when your reply settles it, the agent closes the card itself, which History shows.
- Changed: When you ask an agent to send you a report or file, it now arrives in Needs you as a card that opens it beside the conversation, where you can comment on it and answer, so it doesn't disappear with a notification.
- Changed: Phones and tablets use less data and battery.
