# Claktory Visual Identity

Graphite surfaces, hairline structure, one signal color, and a color for each agent. Claktory should feel like a precise instrument: quiet until something matters.

**Name.** The product is **Claktory** (claktory.com); and the build agents try changes in, **Claktory Dev**. That's the name in everything the person sees: the menu bar, About, the Dock and Finder, window titles and the app's text. Internals keep their old name so installs and updates never move: the bundle is still Factory.app (Factory Dev.app) with its bundle id, macOS showing the name from its localized display name, and the data folder, ports and protocol stay `factory`.

## Principles

1. **Content first, chrome recedes.** Neutral graphite carries all structure. The UI frames the work and never competes with it.
2. **Hairlines, not shadows.** Regions are separated by 1px lines and small shifts in tone. Shadows are only for things that float above the page (menus, popovers, dialogs).
3. **Color means something.** Signal orange marks selection, focus, live activity and what waits for the person's answer; it is never decoration and never a large fill. Each agent has its own color, which says whose something is: its bar, its character, and the bubbles of messages to and from it. Web links are `link` blue, so they stand out from the text around them.
4. **Motion confirms changes.** Animations are short, physical and interruptible. Changes the user causes animate; content streaming in from agents does not, except that a whole new conversation entry eases into view rather than popping in. Agent characters may show quiet life through eyes and an almost still seated breath. Identity stays put. Work remains the working wave, not a dance. Reduced motion freezes the face in its current mood.
5. **Same everywhere.** The app looks identical on macOS, Linux and Windows. Only the window controls are native.

Contrast (WCAG): `text` and `textMuted` are at least 7:1 on both surfaces. `textFaint` is 3.1–4:1, so it's only for non-essential text; a phone has its own, at least 4.5:1 (see Phone). `signal` is at least 3.6:1 against both surfaces. Text placed on a `signal` fill is black (5.3–6.8:1). `danger` is at least 5.2:1 on both surfaces, and its rose hue keeps it distinct from `signal`. `link` is at least 5.3:1 on both surfaces; its blue is far from `signal`'s orange and `danger`'s rose.

## Typography

- **UI:** Geist. **Code and agent output:** Geist Mono. Both are SIL OFL licensed and bundled with the app (`app/fonts/`), so text renders the same on every OS.
- **Sizes:** 12 (caption), 13 (body, the default), 15 (emphasis), 20 (title), 28 (display).
- **Weights:** 400 regular, 500 for labels and controls, 600 for titles. Nothing heavier.
- **Line height:** 1.45 for body, 1.2 for titles.

## Space, shape and depth

- **Spacing:** 4px grid. Scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48.
- **Radii:** 6 for controls, 10 for panels and cards, 14 for floating layers.
- **Borders:** 1px `hairline`. No double borders: adjacent regions share a single line.
- **Shadows:** floating layers only. One soft shadow, no colored glows.

## Motion

| Token | Duration | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `fast` | 120 ms | Hover, press, toggles |
| `base` | 200 ms | Panels, sidebars, reveals |
| `slow` | 320 ms | Full-view transitions |

- Entering motion decelerates (ease-out). Exits run at 75% of the enter duration.
- Dragging, resizing and other direct manipulation uses critically damped springs that follow the pointer and settle without overshoot.
- All motion can be interrupted mid-flight and retargeted.
- Streamed content (tokens, logs, progress) appears in place with no animation. Layout must not jump while it arrives.
- A new conversation entry (any kind: replies, events, tool calls, notices, the person's own message) arrives smoothly: while the view follows the bottom, it glides there over 300ms (ease-out cubic), retargeting as the entry grows, while the entry fades in and rises 8px in `base`. Text streaming into an entry is followed instantly. Opening an agent lands at the bottom at once, an entry is never animated twice, and a view the person has scrolled up stays where it is; scrolling during a glide ends it.
- A queued message shows after the last entry as it will look delivered, at 50% opacity, with "Queued" under it in caption, faint, on its side. Delivered, its entry takes the same place and brightens from 50% to full in `base` without rising. One that leaves the queue undelivered (Stop) stays 1.5s for its entry, then fades out in `fast`.
- A long conversation opens at its latest entries; earlier ones load as the view nears its top (within 1200px), going in above what's on screen so nothing moves. While they load, a 48px row at the top says "Loading earlier messages…" in caption, faint, after a faint live dot; if they can't, "Couldn't load earlier messages." and why in caption `danger`, then Retry in caption weight 500, `textMuted`.
- **Scrollbars:** a 6px thumb, 3px radius, 2px in from the edge, in `textFaint` at 50%, with no track; it shows while scrolling and fades out 600ms after, staying while the pointer's on it or it's dragged. In a conversation it's placed by the heights of every entry seen so far (the ones not seen yet at their average; earlier pages counted by their seqs), never moves against the scroll, and is at the top and the bottom at the very start and end, so it moves steadily however different the entries' heights. Dragging it scrolls by the same share; a click in its track pages toward the click.
- **Latest:** while the conversation is scrolled up from its true end, on a Mac a 28px floating button at its bottom right, 16px in and 12px above the message box's area (`sidebar` fill, `selected` on hover, hairline, 14px radius, the floating shadow) with a 14px Lucide arrow-down in `textMuted` (`text` on hover), and, once replies or agents' messages came in since it left the end, "3 new" in label 500 `text`, tabular, 10px in, before the arrow; it fades in and out in `fast` ("3 new messages, go to the latest" to VoiceOver). A click glides to the end and follows it again. On a phone it's the Latest pill at the foot (see Phone). It sits apart from the Needs you chip at the top right.
- A message the person sends shows at once, before the backend has it: after what's queued, like a queued one, rising in as a new entry does, with "Sending…" in place of "Queued". The backend's own takes its place without moving: queued, it keeps "Sending…" for a second (an idle agent takes it at once), then says "Queued"; delivered, it brightens as above. One that isn't sent (the connection dropped, or the backend refused it) stays at 50% with "Not sent" in caption `danger` under it (why in a hover card, or when held), then Retry and Edit in caption weight 500, `textMuted` (`text` on hover); Edit takes it back to the message box, after anything typed there.
- **Highlights:** a glow over what changed or was pointed out: a fill of its color at 22% across the target's shape (its radius) and a 12px halo at 40%, together fading in over 0.3s (ease-out), holding 0.3s and fading out over 0.8s (ease-in-out), 1.4s in all, no movement or bounce. Two agents' colors (a message) run as a left-to-right gradient, the halo their blend. Agents glow in their own color; what isn't an agent's (projects, folders, Settings, the Marketplace) in signal. With reduced motion it's the same light, still, at 70% for 0.9s. A callout is a floating layer (sidebar color, hairline, 10px radius, the floating shadow) 8px beside the first target, caption in `text`, at most 260px wide; it fades in and out in `base` and goes after 5s or on a click.
- **Working wave:** while an agent works, a soft band of its color (16% at its middle, fading to nothing at both ends, 55% of the row wide and at least 80px) glides across its sidebar row behind the content, left to right and back again without pause, 1.8s each way, easing into each turn. Its middle turns at the row's edge, so at least half of it is always in view: a working agent never looks idle. Every wave keeps one shared clock, so several working agents sweep together as one calm rhythm. A collapsed agent waves for a working agent folded under it; a folded folder heading waves in `text` at 7%. An agent waiting out usage limits doesn't wave, here or on its header. Along the agent's header, a white stretch at 60% runs back and forth over its 2px colored line in step. The character stays in its cell; its eyes never follow the wave.
- **Rotating hint:** a message box hint of several lines shows one at a time, in the placeholder's place and style: every 3.5s the line slides up and out as the next slides up into its place, each moving one line height over 450ms (ease-in-out cubic) and fading as it goes or comes, clipped to the line. It holds still while the pointer is over the box, and hides with the placeholder once there's text. With reduced motion the lines simply swap.
- **Agent characters:** each agent is a small living character: a plain geometric shape filled flat with its color (no outline, shadow or gradient) with two white capsule eyes, the same eyes on every agent, inside a 4% margin of its square (the circle 6%, since a circle looks bigger at the same width). The shape (circle, square, triangle pointing up, diamond, pentagon point up, or flat-topped hexagon) is its identity, resolved by Claktory, and never changes with its mood; the polygons' corners are softened by a radius of 5% of the square, so each reads as the polygon it is. The eyes sit a little above the middle, lower and a touch smaller where a shape narrows toward its top (triangle, diamond, pentagon). Choosing another shape in settings blinks once while the body eases into it over 250ms (ease-in-out cubic), the eyes moving to their new place while they're shut. Its mood shows only in the eyes. Calm moods move only in short shared windows: every 3.5s, for 0.4s, some characters blink (180ms, the eyes' height easing to a sliver and back) or glance (a new place within ±4%, eased over 300ms), each at its own moment in the window, and hold perfectly still in between. Idle blinks and glances, each about every 10s, with a seated breath of ±0.5% height over 6s anchored at its base that advances only in the windows; thinking (moving all the time, not only in windows), a slow look up and back over about 3s with a tiny lid lift; tool running (likewise), small glances down toward the work every 2.5s; news, pleased crescent eyes, with one blink and a brief widening (400ms) when it arrives; failed, gaze down under drooped lids, blinking; no model, half-lidded and open, blinking rarely (about every 20s), no breath; asleep, closed arcs and the breath alone. Lids are drawn in the body's color, never a darker shade. A change of mood blends from the face as it is over 220ms (ease-out cubic), so quick changes stay continuous; a window that opens draws the mood as it is, replaying nothing. Every agent runs at its own phase, so a sidebar of them never blinks together, and every copy of one agent shares one pose. Live characters (sidebar and Messaging at 24px, the header at 34px, the settings preview at 80px) show the mood; references (beside messages at 22px; the back chip, owners, agent pickers and panels at 20px) keep the resting eyes and only blink, in the windows, about every 17s. They move from one clock per window: 30 times a second for a thinking or working face bigger than 18px (or one changing mood), 20 for the rest. While a working wave or a live dot moves anywhere in the window, they move on its clock instead, 25 times a second, so faces and waves draw in the same frames: every frame redraws the whole window, so clocks out of step would add frames. Clocks that count seconds (a tool call's) tick together on that clock too, or on one shared timer when nothing else moves. They redraw only while on screen and only once a face has moved a tenth of a pixel; between calm windows, with nobody working, the clock sleeps and the window draws nothing. It stops while the window is hidden. A deleted or unknown agent is a still circle in `textFaint` with canvas-colored eyes. A character is never the only sign of a state: names, status words, the wave and the unseen marker stay.
- The text caret blinks only while Claktory's window is the active one; in a window in the background it hides, as in any Mac app, so a Claktory left open draws nothing while nothing happens.
- With the system's reduced motion on, the conversation's glides and arrivals are instant, the working wave is a still tint of the agent's color across the row (10%), with nothing on the header, and every character holds its mood's still face (references their resting face): no gaze, blink, breath or flourish.
- The system's reduced motion is iOS's and Android's own setting, and on a Mac System Settings → Accessibility → Display → Reduce motion, which the window watches itself (Flutter doesn't pass it on there). While no one can see the window (covered by other windows, minimized or hidden, or the app in the background on a phone or tablet), nothing in it moves by itself: no wave, face, caret or counting clock draws until it shows again.

## Layout

- **Window:** minimum 800 × 500. The first launch opens centered at about 78% × 85% of the screen (at most 1440 × 940); after that it reopens at its last size and position, kept on the device (tenet 7's one exception). On macOS the title bar is transparent, and the traffic lights sit over the sidebar, centered in its top row 16px from the left.
- **Title bar zone:** the top 44px of the window. Over the sidebar it holds the window controls and nothing else; over the content and the extension panel, their header bars fill it, so there's no empty band above them. All of it drags the window, and double-clicking a spot that isn't a control does what the system's title bar double-click does (zoom by default). A view without a header bar keeps the 44px band empty.
- **Claktory rail:** 40px (`railWidth`) at the window's far left, in the sidebar's color. Inside, this Mac's is a 16px Lucide laptop-minimal and any other its name's initials (up to two, "mac-mini" → "MM") in caption weight 600, both `textMuted` (`text` when selected or hovered, `textFaint` while not connected).
