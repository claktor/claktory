# Claktory Visual Identity

Graphite surfaces, hairline structure, one signal color, and a color for each agent. Claktory should feel like a precise instrument: quiet until something matters.

**Name.** The product is **Claktory** (claktory.com); and the build agents try changes in, **Claktory Dev**. That's the name in everything the person sees: the menu bar, About, the Dock and Finder, window titles and the app's text. Internals keep their old name so installs and updates never move: the bundle is still Factory.app (Factory Dev.app) with its bundle id, macOS showing the name from its localized display name, and the data folder, ports and protocol stay `factory`.

## Principles

1. **Content first, chrome recedes.** Neutral graphite carries all structure. The UI frames the work and never competes with it.
2. **Hairlines, not shadows.** Regions are separated by 1px lines and small shifts in tone. Shadows are only for things that float above the page (menus, popovers, dialogs).
3. **Color means something.** Signal orange marks selection, focus and live activity; it is never decoration and never a large fill. Each agent has its own color, which says whose something is: its bar, its character, and the bubbles of messages to and from it.
4. **Motion confirms changes.** Animations are short, physical and interruptible. Changes the user causes animate; content streaming in from agents does not, except that a whole new conversation entry eases into view rather than popping in. Agent characters may show quiet life through eyes and an almost still seated breath. Identity stays put. Work remains the working wave, not a dance. Reduced motion freezes the face in its current mood.
5. **Same everywhere.** The app looks identical on macOS, Linux and Windows. Only the window controls are native.

## Color

The app follows the system light/dark setting. Both themes use the same roles.

| Role | Dark | Light | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `canvas` | `#0E0E10` | `#FFFFFF` | Main content area |
| `sidebar` | `#151517` | `#F5F5F6` | Sidebar and secondary panels |
| `hairline` | `#232327` | `#E4E4E7` | All dividers and borders |
| `text` | `#EDEDEF` | `#18181B` | Primary text |
| `textMuted` | `#A1A1AA` | `#52525B` | Secondary text, labels |
| `textFaint` | `#71717A` | `#8B8B94` | Hints, timestamps; never essential information |
| `signal` | `#FF5B1F` | `#E5480F` | Selection, focus, live state |
| `danger` | `#FF5C7A` | `#C8193C` | Errors and failed actions only |

Agent colors are the same in both themes. Each fills bubbles with white text on it, at least 4.5:1. A new agent gets the color that looks least like any agent's color (furthest in Oklab), so agents stay as distinct as they can be; the backend picks it (`config.rs`, which repeats these values):

| Name | Value |
|---|---|
| `blue` | `#2F6FEB` |
| `violet` | `#7B52E0` |
| `green` | `#1F8549` |
| `pink` | `#C7358A` |
| `teal` | `#11808A` |
| `amber` | `#A86009` |
| `red` | `#D03C3C` |
| `slate` | `#5A6377` |

Contrast (WCAG): `text` and `textMuted` are at least 7:1 on both surfaces. `textFaint` is 3.1–4:1, so it's only for non-essential text. `signal` is at least 3.6:1 against both surfaces. Text placed on a `signal` fill is black (5.3–6.8:1). `danger` is at least 5.2:1 on both surfaces, and its rose hue keeps it distinct from `signal`.

## Typography

- **UI:** Geist. **Code and agent output:** Geist Mono. Both are SIL OFL licensed and bundled with the app (`app/fonts/`), so text renders the same on every OS.
- **Sizes:** 12 (caption), 13 (body, the default), 15 (emphasis), 20 (title), 28 (display).
- **Weights:** 400 regular, 500 for labels and controls, 600 for titles. Nothing heavier.
- **Line height:** 1.45 for body, 1.2 for titles.

## Space, shape and depth

- **Spacing:** 4px grid. Scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48.
- **Radii:** 6 for controls, 10 for panels and cards, 14 for floating layers.
- **Borders:** 1px `hairline`. No double borders: adjacent regions share a single line.
- **Shadows:** floating layers only. One soft shadow, no colored glows.

## Motion

| Token | Duration | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `fast` | 120 ms | Hover, press, toggles |
| `base` | 200 ms | Panels, sidebars, reveals |
| `slow` | 320 ms | Full-view transitions |

- Entering motion decelerates (ease-out). Exits run at 75% of the enter duration.
- Dragging, resizing and other direct manipulation uses critically damped springs that follow the pointer and settle without overshoot.
- All motion can be interrupted mid-flight and retargeted.
- Streamed content (tokens, logs, progress) appears in place with no animation. Layout must not jump while it arrives.
- A new conversation entry (any kind: replies, events, tool calls, notices, the person's own message) arrives smoothly: while the view follows the bottom, it glides there over 300ms (ease-out cubic), retargeting as the entry grows, while the entry fades in and rises 8px in `base`. Text streaming into an entry is followed instantly. Opening an agent lands at the bottom at once, an entry is never animated twice, and a view the person has scrolled up stays where it is; scrolling during a glide ends it.
- A queued message shows after the last entry as it will look delivered, at 50% opacity, with "Queued" under it in caption, faint, on its side. Delivered, its entry takes the same place and brightens from 50% to full in `base` without rising. One that leaves the queue undelivered (Stop) stays 1.5s for its entry, then fades out in `fast`.
- **Highlights:** a glow over what changed or was pointed out: a fill of its color at 22% across the target's shape (its radius) and a 12px halo at 40%, together fading in over 0.3s (ease-out), holding 0.3s and fading out over 0.8s (ease-in-out), 1.4s in all, no movement or bounce. Two agents' colors (a message) run as a left-to-right gradient, the halo their blend. Agents glow in their own color; what isn't an agent's (projects, folders, Settings, the Marketplace) in signal. With reduced motion it's the same light, still, at 70% for 0.9s. A callout is a floating layer (sidebar color, hairline, 10px radius, the floating shadow) 8px beside the first target, caption in `text`, at most 260px wide; it fades in and out in `base` and goes after 5s or on a click.
- **Working wave:** while an agent works, a soft band of its color (16% at its middle, fading to nothing at both ends, 55% of the row wide and at least 80px) glides across its sidebar row behind the content, left to right and back again without pause, 1.8s each way, easing into each turn. Its middle turns at the row's edge, so at least half of it is always in view: a working agent never looks idle. Every wave keeps one shared clock, so several working agents sweep together as one calm rhythm. A collapsed agent waves for a working agent folded under it; a folded folder heading waves in `text` at 7%. Along the agent's header, a white stretch at 60% runs back and forth over its 2px colored line in step. The character stays in its cell; its eyes never follow the wave.
- **Rotating hint:** a message box hint of several lines shows one at a time, in the placeholder's place and style: every 3.5s the line slides up and out as the next slides up into its place, each moving one line height over 450ms (ease-in-out cubic) and fading as it goes or comes, clipped to the line. It holds still while the pointer is over the box, and hides with the placeholder once there's text. With reduced motion the lines simply swap.
- **Agent characters:** each agent is a small living character: a plain geometric shape filled flat with its color (no outline, shadow or gradient) with two white capsule eyes, the same eyes on every agent, inside a 4% margin of its square (the circle 6%, since a circle looks bigger at the same width). The shape (circle, square, triangle pointing up, diamond, pentagon point up, or flat-topped hexagon) is its identity, resolved by Claktory, and never changes with its mood; the polygons' corners are softened by a radius of 5% of the square, so each reads as the polygon it is. The eyes sit a little above the middle, lower and a touch smaller where a shape narrows toward its top (triangle, diamond, pentagon). Choosing another shape in settings blinks once while the body eases into it over 250ms (ease-in-out cubic), the eyes moving to their new place while they're shut. Its mood shows only in the eyes. Calm moods move only in short shared windows: every 3.5s, for 0.4s, some characters blink (180ms, the eyes' height easing to a sliver and back) or glance (a new place within ±4%, eased over 300ms), each at its own moment in the window, and hold perfectly still in between. Idle blinks and glances, each about every 10s, with a seated breath of ±0.5% height over 6s anchored at its base that advances only in the windows; thinking (moving all the time, not only in windows), a slow look up and back over about 3s with a tiny lid lift; tool running (likewise), small glances down toward the work every 2.5s; news, pleased crescent eyes, with one blink and a brief widening (400ms) when it arrives; failed, gaze down under drooped lids, blinking; no model, half-lidded and open, blinking rarely (about every 20s), no breath; asleep, closed arcs and the breath alone. Lids are drawn in the body's color, never a darker shade. A change of mood blends from the face as it is over 220ms (ease-out cubic), so quick changes stay continuous; a window that opens draws the mood as it is, replaying nothing. Every agent runs at its own phase, so a sidebar of them never blinks together, and every copy of one agent shares one pose. Live characters (sidebar and Messaging at 24px, the header at 34px, the settings preview at 80px) show the mood; references (beside messages at 22px; the back chip, owners, agent pickers and panels at 20px) keep the resting eyes and only blink, in the windows, about every 17s. They move from one clock per window: 30 times a second for a thinking or working face bigger than 18px (or one changing mood), 20 for the rest. They redraw only while on screen and only once a face has moved a tenth of a pixel; between calm windows, with nobody working, the clock sleeps and the window draws nothing. It stops while the window is hidden. A deleted or unknown agent is a still circle in `textFaint` with canvas-colored eyes. A character is never the only sign of a state: names, status words, the wave and the unseen marker stay.
- The text caret blinks only while Claktory's window is the active one; in a window in the background it hides, as in any Mac app, so a Claktory left open draws nothing while nothing happens.
- With the system's reduced motion on, the conversation's glides and arrivals are instant, the working wave is a still tint of the agent's color across the row (10%), with nothing on the header, and every character holds its mood's still face (references their resting face): no gaze, blink, breath or flourish.

## Layout

- **Window:** minimum 800 × 500. The first launch opens centered at about 78% × 85% of the screen (at most 1440 × 940); after that it reopens at its last size and position, kept on the device (tenet 7's one exception). On macOS the title bar is transparent, and the traffic lights sit over the sidebar, centered in its top row 16px from the left.
- **Title bar zone:** the top 44px of the window. Over the sidebar it holds the window controls and nothing else; over the content and the explorer, their header bars fill it, so there's no empty band above them. All of it drags the window, and double-clicking a spot that isn't a control does what the system's title bar double-click does (zoom by default). A view without a header bar keeps the 44px band empty.
- **Sidebar:** 248px by default, resizable between 200 and 360. The width is workspace state, so it lives in the backend like all other state.
- **Focused project:** Rivet's row (the admin agent's) stays at the top, as it stands over all projects, 8px above "‹ All projects", then the project's heading and its agents. It's the same row as anywhere, wave, unseen mark and all; with no admin agent it isn't there.
- **Agent rows:** 32px tall (other sidebar rows are 28px): the agent's 24px live character with 6px after it (the working wave says it works), its name in `textMuted` (`text` and weight 500 when selected), and its queued count, faint. With a role the row is 40px and the role sits under the name: caption at 11px, line height 1.25 (the name's too, so the two sit close), faint (`textMuted` when selected or hovered), one line with an ellipsis; without one the row is as it was. When a run ended since the person last opened it, the name is `text` at weight 600 and a 7px dot of its color ends the row, until it's opened. It appears without animation, since the agent caused it.
- **Nesting:** agents nested under another sit 16px further in. Rows in a place with nesting keep a 14px gutter before the character for the disclosure, a 10px chevron (`textFaint`, `textMuted` on hover) that turns in `fast`; the nested rows open and close in `base`. A collapsed agent pulses and shows the unseen marker for its hidden agents; its character keeps its own mood.
- **Dragging agents:** dropping between rows shows a 2px `signal` line at the row's edge, indented to the level it would land at; dropping onto a row (nesting) rings the row in `signal`; dropping onto a place rings the place, as moving between places always has.
- **Ask Rivet "+":** a 20px quiet button, 6px radius, with a 14px Lucide plus in `textFaint` (`textMuted` and the hover tint on hover) and "Ask Rivet to set it up" in a hover card.
